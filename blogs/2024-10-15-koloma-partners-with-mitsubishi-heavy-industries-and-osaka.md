Koloma, a leading geologic hydrogen exploration company, announced today a strategic investment from Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Americas, Inc. and Osaka Gas Co., Ltd. Both partnerships mark a significant step toward harnessing geologic hydrogen as a clean, abundant energy source, with global decarbonization efforts at the forefront.
